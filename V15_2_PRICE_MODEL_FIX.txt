Cashew Creek Villa V15.2 - Price Model Fix

There are exactly TWO prices in the system:
1. Customer Price (menu_items.selling_price) - shown on Order, customer bills and customer-facing printouts.
2. Supplier Cost (supplier_item_costs.supplier_rate) - owner-only purchase cost, used only for supplier bills.

The ordering screen never reads or displays supplier cost.
The customer bill uses order_items.selling_price.
The supplier bill uses supplier_bill_items.supplier_rate.

Important:
- Repair / Load Master Menu updates CUSTOMER PRICES only.
- It does NOT overwrite or invent supplier costs.
- Maintain supplier costs explicitly in Owner -> Menu.
- The included v15_master_menu_price_correction.sql updates only customer prices and does not modify historical order_items.
