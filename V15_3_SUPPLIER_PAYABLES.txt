V15.5 SUPPLIER PAYABLE WORKFLOW

Supplier bills now have a real payable workflow:
- New supplier bill starts as Pending.
- Owner can use Mark Paid.
- Payment method is Cash or UPI.
- Optional payment reference can be stored.
- Paid timestamp and the owner who marked it paid are stored.
- Supplier screen shows Pending and Paid totals/counts.
- Supplier bill detail and print show payment details when paid.
- Re-running supplier-bill creation no longer resets an already-paid bill back to Pending.

Run v15_3_supplier_payments_migration.sql after the earlier V15 supplier/RPC migrations.

V15.5 hotfix:
- Fixed supplier bill creation error caused by inserting into generated line_total.
- Supplier bill item line_total is now calculated by the database.
